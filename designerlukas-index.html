03 / About

I'm Lukas. I co-founded Overture, a clipping agency, and I spend most of my time in the timeline, cutting short-form and figuring out why one clip travels and a near identical one doesn't.

Short-form is a volume game, but volume alone is worthless. What matters is the first second, the pacing underneath it and knowing which thirty seconds of a two hour conversation were ever worth posting. That's the part I care about.

Before this I worked as a web designer and still build the occasional site, which is why you're reading this on designerlukas.com.
